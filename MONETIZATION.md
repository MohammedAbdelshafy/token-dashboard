# Token Dashboard Commercialization

## Product
Local-first AI cost and workflow observability for Claude Code users and small engineering teams.

## Buyer outcome
Understand token/cost drivers, expensive prompts, tool-result bloat, project-level usage, cache behavior, and recurring waste without sending prompt data to a remote service.

## Packaging
- Free/local edition remains the acquisition channel.
- Paid option can bundle team policy packs, export/reporting, onboarding, and managed optimization only after validating demand.
- Do not change the privacy promise without an explicit product decision.

## Proof
Use benchmark fixtures and measured local scans, not fabricated savings claims.
